What we collect

When you join the Doco waitlist, we collect the email address you submit, the signup source, and the time of signup. We also use limited technical information to protect the form from abuse.

We use your email only to manage the waitlist and send launch or availability updates about Doco. We do not sell your information.

Waitlist information is stored with Supabase. We retain it while Doco is preparing for launch and for a reasonable period afterward, unless you ask us to remove it sooner.
