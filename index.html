A few questions.

What is Doco?

An expressive little companion for focus, music and everyday moments.

Do I need a Gemini key?

No. Doco has expressions and prompts without one. Add your own Gemini API key for camera-based reactions and AI captions.

What changes in Focus mode?

Everyday is playful and warm. Focus brings a little less distraction—and a little more friendly sarcasm.

How does the music work?

Connect Spotify to play music in Doco. Song selection stays close to your listening history, with room for something new.

Can Doco visit my computer?

Yes. Install the Windows client, sign in with the same Google account, and enable visits on both devices.
